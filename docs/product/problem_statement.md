# MaintMind Product Problem Statement

Maintenance teams generate work orders, technician notes, inspection records, equipment histories, telemetry, and technical documents across disconnected systems and inconsistent formats. This fragmentation makes it difficult to identify recurring failures, assess equipment risk, retrieve current procedures, distinguish reliable evidence from unsupported assumptions, and explain how maintenance recommendations were produced. MaintMind addresses this problem by transforming governed maintenance data and approved technical documents into structured, traceable, and evidence-grounded decision support while preserving source provenance, applying deterministic safety policies, requiring human approval for consequential actions, and recording an auditable history of every recommendation and decision.

## Product Objective

MaintMind will provide a reliable maintenance investigation workflow that combines:

* validated maintenance reports
* equipment and work-order history
* deterministic recurrence and risk rules
* predictive risk signals where valid
* structured maintenance-text extraction
* approved technical documentation
* similar historical incidents
* evidence-grounded recommendations
* human approval and feedback
* complete audit records

## Primary Product Outcome

A maintenance planner should be able to submit or review a maintenance report and receive a structured investigation containing:

* extracted equipment, component, symptom, and action information
* missing or inconsistent information
* recurrence and historical context
* deterministic risk and safety flags
* relevant current procedures and supporting evidence
* similar historical incidents
* an evidence-grounded draft recommendation
* explicit uncertainty and limitations
* a mandatory approval state
* a traceable audit record

## Safety Boundary

MaintMind is a decision-support platform.

It will not autonomously:

* authorise maintenance activity
* close work orders
* change safety or risk policies
* modify equipment history
* schedule safety-critical work
* execute recommendations

Consequential actions must be approved by an authorised human reviewer.

## Evidence Standard

A recommendation is not valid merely because it was generated by an AI model.

Every recommendation must be supported by:

* validated source data
* explicit deterministic rules
* approved and versioned documents
* traceable citations
* model, prompt, rule, and dataset versions
* documented uncertainty
* a recorded human decision

## Scope-Control Statement

MaintMind has one primary technical story:

> Build, evaluate, secure, deploy, and operate an evidence-grounded maintenance investigation platform with deep RAG and one justified agentic workflow.

Technologies will only be added when they provide measurable value to this workflow.
